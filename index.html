About Us

Keeping the people you love where they thrive: at home.

Caring for a loved one with medical needs, a disability or the challenges of aging is a big responsibility. Enoeht Home Care exists so families don't have to carry it alone. Our nurses and caregivers provide skilled, hands-on support at home, surrounded by the people and routines your loved one knows.

Enoeht Home Care is a Georgia private home care provider licensed by the Department of Community Health (License PHCP045982). We are a locally owned agency serving families in Cobb, Cherokee, Clayton, DeKalb, Douglas, Fulton, Gwinnett, Henry, Newton and Rockdale counties, and we currently provide care on a private-pay basis.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, skilled and compassionate care that helps children and adults live fuller lives at home.

Our Vision

A Georgia where every family caring for a loved one has dependable, high-quality support at home.

Our Promise to Families

Clear communication, reliable scheduling, and caregivers who treat your loved one like family.

Our Services

Care that fits your family's needs

From skilled nursing to a helping hand with daily routines, every care plan is built around your loved one's needs, physician's orders and family schedule.

Skilled Nursing

Private duty nursing by RNs and LPNs, including medication administration, tube feedings and health monitoring.

Pediatric Care

In-home nursing and personal care support for children with medical needs, coordinated with your child's physician.

Personal Care

Help with bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, mobility and other daily activities, delivered with dignity and respect.

Companion & Sitter Services

Friendly companionship, supervision, light housekeeping, meal preparation and medication reminders.

Respite Care

Short-term relief for family caregivers, so you can rest, work or take care of yourself, knowing your loved one is in good hands.

Care Coordination

We work with physicians, specialists, case managers and families so everyone stays on the same page.

Payment: private pay. We do not accept Medicaid or insurance at this time.

FAQ

Questions families ask us

Who do you serve?

We serve children and adults who need skilled nursing, personal care or companionship at home. We currently serve Cobb, Cherokee, Clayton, DeKalb, Douglas, Fulton, Gwinnett, Henry, Newton and Rockdale counties.

How is care paid for?

Care is currently provided on a private-pay basis. We do not accept Medicaid or insurance at this time. We'll talk through your options and costs during your free consultation.

How many hours of care can we get?

Private-pay care can be scheduled from a few hours a week up to full shifts.

Will we have the same caregiver each time?

We work hard to assign a consistent team so your child and family can build trust with familiar caregivers.

How do I get started?

Call us or fill out the form below. We'll schedule a free consultation, usually within 1–2 business days.
